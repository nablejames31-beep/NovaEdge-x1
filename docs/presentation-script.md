# NovaEdge X1: Presentation Script (5 presenters)

**Total time:** about 10–11 minutes, plus Q&A
**Site:** https://nablejames31-beep.github.io/NovaEdge-x1/

Replace **Presenter 1–5** with your names. Lines in *italics* are actions on the laptop, not words to say.

---

## Before you start (5 minutes before)

- [ ] Laptop is on power, not battery. Open the site in **Chrome or Edge** and press **Ctrl + F5** once so it loads the newest version.
- [ ] Press **F** for fullscreen. Turn off notifications (Windows: Focus assist / Do not disturb).
- [ ] Make sure the internet works. **Backup:** download a screen recording of the site, or run it locally (see README) in case the Wi-Fi fails.
- [ ] Choose **one "driver"** who handles the laptop the whole time, or pass a presentation clicker between speakers.
  A clicker works because it sends the same keys as the keyboard.
- [ ] Scroll back to the very top (**Home** key).

### Controls cheat sheet (for the driver)

| Do this | Result |
|---|---|
| **→**, **Space** or **Page Down** (or clicker "next") | Glide to the next section |
| **←** or **Page Up** (or clicker "back") | Go back one section |
| Mouse wheel / trackpad | Free, smooth scrolling |
| **Click a part** of the phone | Camera flies in, info panel opens |
| **Drag** while zoomed in | Orbit around the part |
| **Scroll** while zoomed in | Zoom in / out |
| **Next → / ← Previous** in the panel | Fly to the next part |
| **Esc** or the **✕** | Zoom back out |
| Click a **photo** | Opens it full screen (click again to close) |
| Click a **color dot** | Phone changes color |
| **F** | Fullscreen on / off |

> And this isn't a video. It's a live 3D model. You can actually look around it.
>
> But the real story is what's inside. **[Presenter 2]** is going to take it apart for us.

## Q&A cheat sheet

Decide in advance who answers which topic. A good split is to let each presenter answer questions about their own section.

| Likely question | Suggested answer | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Is this a real phone? | "NovaEdge X1 is our product concept. The specs show what we believe a flagship should offer next. The 3D experience you saw is fully interactive." | P1 |
| Why titanium? | "Titanium is stronger and lighter than steel, so the phone is durable and still only 196 grams." | P1 |
| Why build your own chip? | "Designing hardware and chip together means better speed and battery life, the same approach the biggest brands use." | P2 |
| What is a TOPS / neural engine? | "TOPS means trillions of operations per second. It measures how fast the phone runs AI features like photo enhancement and translation, right on the device." | P2 |
| Do people really need 200 MP? | "You don't always shoot at full resolution, but it lets you crop and zoom without losing detail, and it combines pixels for brighter low-light shots." | P3 |
| Is 100 W charging safe for the battery? | "Charging is fastest at low battery and slows down as it fills, and the silicon-carbon cell is designed for it, which protects battery health." | P4 |
| Why $1,199? | "It's positioned against other flagships, with more storage, a bigger battery and longer software support for the price." | P5 |
| Who is it for? | "Creators, professionals and students, anyone who uses their phone to work, create and stay connected all day." | P5 |

## Rehearsal tips

1. **Rehearse with the real site** at least twice, with the same person driving each time.
2. Keep each hand-off to one sentence. The "**[Name], …?**" lines are your cue to switch.
3. **Wait for the animation** after each **→** (about 2–3 seconds) before talking about what's on screen.
4. If something freezes, press **Esc**, then use **→ / ←** to get back. If the site won't load, switch to your backup video.
5. Speak to the audience, not the screen. The driver watches the screen for you.
